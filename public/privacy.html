HDS Chat

Pilot service information · 21 October 2026. This app provides a direct chat with HDS and William, its AI sales assistant. It is not WhatsApp and is not a peer-to-peer messaging service.

Information used

Your verified cellphone number links your HDS customer profile, messages, quotes and invoices. We process the text, photos, documents, voice notes and location you choose to send. Location is requested only when you choose to share it; the app does not continuously track you.

Who processes messages

William and HDS staff can process your conversations to answer questions, prepare quotes and help with orders. The service uses HDS's existing hosting, database, automation and AI providers. Messages are encrypted in transit, but are not end-to-end encrypted: server-side processing is necessary for William and staff to respond.

Payments

Checkout opens the existing payment provider pages. Do not send card numbers, passwords, PINs or verification codes in chat. The HDS chat app does not collect card details.

Device storage and notifications

The app caches messages and unsent attachments on your device so chat can reopen offline. Sign out before lending or selling your device. Notifications are optional and may display a message preview on your lock screen. The Android pilot does not yet support native push notifications; installed-web-app notification support varies by browser.

Access and requests

You can sign out this device or revoke other active sessions from Account. For a personal-information access, correction or deletion request, ask the HDS team in chat or use the contact details on the HDS website. HDS may need to retain business transaction records separately. Automatic self-service export/deletion and a final business-approved retention schedule are not yet available in this pilot.

Marketing and pilot limitations

Using chat does not grant unlimited marketing consent. You can tell the HDS team to stop marketing messages; a bulk migration campaign has not been launched. Phone-code delivery independent of WhatsApp requires a configured SMS provider. Availability, payment settlement notifications, installation and push must be checked in the staff pilot before wider rollout.
